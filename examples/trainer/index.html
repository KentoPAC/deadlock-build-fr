How it is computed

The games are real Ascendant+ games from the last two weeks (deadlock-api), frozen at 15 or 20 min. The "best" item is picked among the counter items (anti-heal, anti-tank, resistances, status resistance) that players of your hero actually buy. The "pro's pick" is the item the player bought in the next two and a half minutes. No player name is shown.
